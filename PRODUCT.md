# PRODUCT.md — Lato Studio

Durable product truth. Visual direction lives in DESIGN.md.

## What it is

Lato Studio is a three-person design studio (Luca Terenzi, Alessio Orlando, Matteo Polito)
that produces YouTube thumbnails and visual packaging. The site is a single-page
portfolio whose job is to win inbound enquiries.

Surface: one landing page. Mode: **Persuade** — the visitor decides and acts, and the
design is itself the product sample.

## Audience

**Primary visitor:** brands and companies that publish on YouTube — a marketing lead,
content manager, or agency-side producer evaluating suppliers, not the creator who
edits their own videos.

Consequence: this visitor is spending someone else's budget and must justify the
choice internally. They need proof (volume, retention, recognisable names), a sense of
reliability (turnaround, revisions, process), and a low-friction way to start a
conversation. Individual creators still arrive and convert, but they are not who the
page is written for.

**Market:** international. English throughout — no Italian strings anywhere in the UI.

**Positioning:** upper tier. Not the cheapest, not luxury-priced. The page should read
as a studio that is busy and selective, never as one that is available.

## What success looks like

In order of value:

1. Contact form submitted.
2. Direct approach — email or Instagram DM.
3. Portfolio seen and remembered; the return visit converts.

Everything on the page is measured against those three. A section that serves none of
them is a candidate for removal.

## Evidence the page can use

- 1000+ thumbnails delivered
- 15M+ combined subscribers across clients
- 5B+ combined views
- 6+ years of experience
- 24–48h typical turnaround, rush available
- Revisions included until approval
- Named clients with real subscriber counts (Ninna e Matti, Michele Molteni,
  Marcello Ascani, Jack Nobile, Hyde, and others)
- 17 attributed testimonials

All figures are client-supplied and must not be inflated or invented. Do not add a
claim that is not on this list without asking.

## Voice

Plain, direct, confident, no hype. Sentence case. Short declaratives. The work carries
the persuasion; the copy stays out of its way. No exclamation marks, no growth-hacking
vocabulary, no emoji.

## Constraints — do not change without explicit instruction

These are settled brand decisions, not defaults awaiting improvement:

- The hero background video
- The glass / blur material system
- The black background
- The three-row portfolio marquee
- Instrument Serif italic as the accent voice
- The current logo
- English copy throughout

Impeccable's craft floor discourages several of these (glass as decoration, pure
black, Inter as display). The brief wins: they stay.

## Known tension

The page currently addresses "YouTube creators" while the primary visitor is a brand
or company. The proof on the page (creator names, subscriber counts) is right for both,
but the framing speaks to only one. Worth resolving in copy — as a decision, not a
silent edit.

## Not in scope

No pricing page, no blog, no client login, no booking calendar. Contact happens through
the form, email, or Instagram.
