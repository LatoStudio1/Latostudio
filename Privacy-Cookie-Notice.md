When you make a choice in the cookie banner, a record of that choice is stored in your browser so that it can be respected on later visits. See section 9.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We process personal data for the following purposes:

**Responding to enquiries and requests for collaboration.** Where your request concerns a quotation or potential professional engagement, processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

**Handling other correspondence.** Processing is based on our legitimate interest in receiving and responding to relevant communications, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, taking your rights and interests into account.

**Delivering and protecting the website.** Necessary technical data is processed for content delivery, troubleshooting and security, based on our legitimate interest in operating a functioning and secure website, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

**Managing cookie preferences.** Recording your consent choices is necessary to comply with the rules on cookies and similar technologies, under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

**Meeting legal obligations.** Where processing is required by applicable law, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

**Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.** Where necessary, processing is based on our legitimate interest in protecting our rights, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Submitting a contact request does not subscribe you to a newsletter or constitute consent to receive promotional communications.

Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 8. Retention

**Enquiries that do not lead to a professional engagement.** We retain messages and related contact details for up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact, to manage enquiries and follow up on postponed proposals or projects. We periodically review these records and delete them from both Formspree and Gmail within this period, or earlier when no longer necessary, unless further retention is required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

**Professional engagements.** If an enquiry leads to a professional engagement, we retain the correspondence and records necessary to manage that engagement for its duration. Afterwards, we retain only the records necessary to meet applicable legal, accounting and tax obligations or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, for the relevant retention or limitation periods.

**Consent preferences.** Your cookie choice is stored in your browser for the period set by the consent tool, after which you will be asked again. You can change or withdraw it at any time.

**Technical and security logs.** Retention of technical and security logs processed by our service providers depends on the relevant service, its operational and security purposes, and applicable legal requirements. Further information is available in the providers' privacy notices linked in this document.

**Deletion requests.** You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting info.latostudio@gmail.com. Requests are assessed in accordance with the GDPR, including any applicable obligations or grounds requiring continued retention.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Other technologies, such as local storage, can also store or access information in your browser.

**Technical cookie for consent preferences.** When you accept, reject or customise your choices in the cookie banner, iubenda stores a technical cookie or equivalent browser storage recording that choice. It is necessary to respect your decision and does not require consent.

**No analytics or advertising trackers.** The website does not use analytics, advertising or profiling cookies.

**External resources.** The resources described in section 5 involve connections to third-party servers. Such a connection does not, by itself, mean that a cookie is installed. Where a resource would require consent, it is blocked by the consent tool until consent is given.

**Managing your choices.** You can review or change your preferences at any time through the "Cookie preferences" link in the website footer. You can also manage or delete cookies and other website data through your browser settings. Blocking certain resources may affect website functionality.

Visiting the website, scrolling a page or submitting an enquiry does not constitute consent.
