## 14. Notice at Collection (California residents)

This section is provided for residents of California, United States, under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA").

**Categories of personal information we collect**

- **Identifiers**: name, email address and IP address.
- **Contents of communications**: the message you send through the contact form or by email, including any attachments you choose to send.
- **Internet or other electronic network activity**: technical information such as browser and device details, requested resources, and request dates and times, processed when your browser loads the website.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information. Please do not send it through the contact form.

**Purposes**

We use this information to respond to enquiries and requests for collaboration, to prepare quotations, to deliver and secure the website, to record your cookie preferences, and to comply with legal obligations. See section 3.

**Sale and sharing**

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined by the CCPA. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

**Retention**

Enquiries that do not lead to a professional engagement are retained for up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact. Records relating to a professional engagement are retained for the duration of the engagement and afterwards only as required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Technical data is retained according to the policies of the relevant service providers. See section 8.

**Your rights**

You may request to know, access, correct or delete your personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Send requests to info.latostudio@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity.

The rest of this notice describes our practices in full.
